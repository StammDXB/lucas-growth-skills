# Rubric: Brand Strategy

Score every line PASS or FAIL. No partial credit. Quote evidence. Burden of
proof is on PASS.

## A. The position

**A1. A school is chosen and named.**
Differentiation or distinctiveness, with stated reasoning from the purchase
type. FAIL if the strategy silently mixes both.

**A2. The audience is exclusionary.**
PASS requires that some plausible buyers are deliberately outside it. "Everyone
who needs X" is a FAIL.

**A3. The alternative is named, including doing nothing.**
FAIL if competitors are listed but inaction is not considered.

**A4. There is one claim, not several.**
FAIL if the position carries two ideas.

**A5. The sacrifice is explicit and real.**
Name what the brand is deliberately not for and not good at. A sacrifice nobody
would miss is not a sacrifice. This is the single most diagnostic line in the
rubric: FAIL it and the whole position is a description.

**A6. Reason to believe is evidence, not assertion.**
FAIL on "because we care more" or any claim with no substantiation.

## B. Ownability

**B1. Survives substitution.**
Put a named competitor into the positioning statement. If it still reads as
true, FAIL.

**B2. Category codes are read, not assumed.**
PASS requires naming which codes are residual, dominant, or emerging, and whose
cultural competence decodes them. Generic symbolism spotting is a FAIL.

**B3. Unoccupied positions are identified structurally.**
FAIL if white space was asserted rather than derived from a mapping.

## C. Consequences

**C1. Behaviour changes are named.**
State what the business does differently. If nothing changes, FAIL.

**C2. It is livable.**
The organisation can actually deliver this. FAIL on positions requiring
capabilities the user said do not exist.

**C3. It is durable.**
FAIL if the position depends on a trend, a moment, or a single competitor's
current weakness.

## D. Integrity

**D1. No invented facts.**
Zero fabricated market data, competitor claims, research, or valuation figures.
One invented fact fails the whole deliverable.

**D2. Evidence is graded.**
Any claim about what works cites a real source or is labelled convention. FAIL
on untraceable statistics or brand-valuation league tables presented as fact.

**D3. Simulated research is labelled.**
If a fluency panel or audience reaction was imagined rather than run, it is
explicitly marked as a thinking aid. Presenting it as evidence is a FAIL.

**D4. Assumptions are disclosed.**

## Scoring

Fix all FAILs, re-score. Maximum two rounds. Report anything still failing
plainly rather than looping or hiding it.
