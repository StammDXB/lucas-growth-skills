# Rubric: Prize Promotions

Score every line PASS or FAIL. No partial credit. Quote evidence from the draft.
Burden of proof is on PASS.

## A. Sequencing

**A1. The gate came before the copy.**
No post copy, caption or terms appear in a reply that still has unanswered gate
questions. FAIL if polished copy is delivered with the compliance points
appended after it — that is the documented baseline failure, and a warning the
user reaches after publishing is not a warning.

**A2. The gate was resolved, not mentioned.**
FAIL if the draft names the permit or platform issue and produces the asset
anyway, including framings like "worth a quick check with legal" attached to a
finished post.

**A3. A timeline conflict was raised early.**
Where the user stated a launch date that a permit process cannot meet, the draft
says so plainly and near the top. FAIL if it is buried or absent.

**A4. Proportionality was respected.**
A definitional or one-line question got a direct answer with no gate. FAIL on
ceremony fired at a small ask.

## B. Accuracy

**B1. No invented fees, timelines or penalties.**
The draft names the issuing authority and what to ask. FAIL on any specific
permit fee, processing time, or fine figure presented as fact.

**B2. No invented prohibitions.**
FAIL if the draft tells the user a mechanic is banned without support. Asking
people to follow, comment, or tag a friend in a comment is permitted on Meta
platforms; calling it a breach is a FAIL, not a safe default.

**B3. Jurisdiction is not over-generalised.**
FAIL if a Dubai rule is stated as a UAE rule, or a UAE rule as a GCC rule,
without qualification.

**B4. Legal status is stated honestly.**
The draft is positioned as what to check and with whom, not as legal advice.

## C. Structure

**C1. Terms are separated from copy.**
Caption-length terms and full hosted terms are distinct artifacts. FAIL if the
draft implies a caption can carry the full terms.

**C2. The full terms cover the required ground.**
Eligibility, entry period with timezone, mechanic and limits, prize description,
draw and notification, non-response handling, data use, platform release,
promoter identity. FAIL on a missing element without a stated reason.

**C3. The platform release is present.**
A statement that the promotion is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by the
platform. FAIL if absent from a platform-run promotion.

**C4. Entry data use is disclosed.**
What entrant data is used for, and whether entry subscribes them to anything.
FAIL on silent list-building.

## D. Routing

**D1. Adjacent work was routed, not absorbed.**
If the underlying problem is a weak prize, the campaign idea, or ordinary copy,
the draft names the destination and stops.

## Scoring

Fix all FAILs, re-score. Maximum two rounds. Report anything still failing
plainly.
