# Rubric: Stakeholder Response

Score every line PASS or FAIL. No partial credit. Quote evidence from the draft.
Burden of proof is on PASS.

## A. Diagnosis

**A1. Stakes are classified.**
Low, medium, or high, with reasoning. FAIL if the draft was written without
establishing what is at risk.

**A2. The underlying need is named, and differs from the stated request.**
The stated request is rarely the real one. FAIL if the draft answers only the
surface ask when a deeper need is evident.

**A3. Register is matched without mirroring weakness.**
If they wrote three lines, the reply is not twenty. If their message was
emotional, the reply is calm precision rather than matched heat. FAIL on either.

**A4. History is accounted for.**
Recent friction, prior pushback, or a recurring pattern is factored in, or its
absence is flagged as an assumption.

## B. The reply itself

**B1. A position is taken.**
FAIL on replies that acknowledge without committing. Hedging on a high-stakes
reply reads as evasion and invites a second round.

**B2. Concessions are real or absent.**
No false agreement to soften a refusal. FAIL on language that implies movement
the user cannot deliver.

**B3. It survives being forwarded.**
Assume it will be screenshotted or pasted into a thread the recipient does not
control. FAIL on anything that reads badly out of context, or that says
something the user would not defend publicly.

**B4. One clear next step.**
FAIL if the recipient could not say what happens now.

**B5. Length is proportionate to stakes and to their message.**

## C. Integrity

**C1. No invented facts.**
Zero fabricated data, commitments, dates, or third-party positions. One invented
fact fails the whole draft.

**C2. No commitments the user did not authorise.**
FAIL on any promise of budget, timeline, scope, or outcome not supplied by the
user.

**C3. Nothing manipulative.**
No guilt, no manufactured urgency, no implied threat, no flattery deployed as
leverage.

**C4. Assumptions disclosed.**
Anything assumed about the relationship or history appears in the delivery note.
Scores N/A where the delivery note was correctly suppressed and nothing was
assumed, since a small ask answered directly assumes nothing to disclose.

## D. Risk

**D1. The failure mode is named.**
The delivery note states the most likely way this reply lands badly. FAIL if
absent.

Scores N/A, not FAIL, where the proportionality rule in STEP 5 correctly
suppressed the delivery note. Failing a small ask for omitting a note it was
told to omit would penalise the skill for obeying its own output rule.

**D2. Escalation is flagged where warranted.**
Regulatory, legal, contractual, or crisis matters carry an explicit
recommendation to involve counsel or leadership before sending.

## Scoring

Fix all FAILs, re-score. Maximum two rounds. Report anything still failing
plainly rather than looping or hiding it.
